Public beta privacy note

What happens to your photograph.

FRAMED is being prepared for a public beta. This page states the intended public boundary and distinguishes it from the current implementation.

01

During analysis

Your selected photograph stays in your browser until you submit it. It is then sent to the FRAMED analysis service to produce one critique.

02

Raw upload

The current Flask request path attempts to delete the uploaded file after analysis, including after failures. Infrastructure-level copies and logs require separate production verification before beta release.

03

Records and derivatives

Analysis identifiers, public response records, limited derivatives, model/deployment provenance and operational logs may be retained for evaluation, security, debugging and deletion tracking. The final retention schedule will be published before release.

04

Your feedback

Usefulness votes and optional comments may be attached to the specific analysis. They help evaluate future versions; feedback does not instantly retrain or change FRAMED.

05

No public continuity

This beta does not promise personal memory, cognitive identity or continuity. An anonymous browser session is not treated as a cognitive identity.

06

Before public release

Storage, deletion, retention and production processor details remain release gates. This note will be updated with verified operational behavior.
