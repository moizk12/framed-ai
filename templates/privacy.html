Public beta privacy note

What happens to your photograph.

Each public critique is a standalone request. Here is what the application processes and stores.

01

During analysis

Your photograph stays in your browser until you submit it. FRAMED processes its pixels and sends the image to the configured recognition provider. Later reasoning and expression receive text evidence only. The provider may be locally hosted or a cloud service; its own processing and retention practices apply.

02

Raw upload

The server uses a temporary file and attempts to delete it when the request finishes, including after failures. A process interruption or deletion failure can leave a temporary file. This does not guarantee deletion from hosting infrastructure or provider systems.

03

Stored records

The public database stores analysis identifiers and creation times, plus feedback attached to those identifiers. It does not store the uploaded image or critique text. Operational logs can contain diagnostic details. Database records persist until removed by the operator; the application has no automatic retention expiry.

04

Your feedback

Usefulness votes and optional comments are stored with the analysis identifier. Avoid personal or sensitive information in comments. Feedback helps evaluate future versions; it does not instantly retrain or change FRAMED.

05

No public continuity

Public analysis does not retrieve or write research memory, track your creative history, or build a personal cognitive identity. Research cognition uses a separate persistence boundary.

06

Service limits

Requests are subject to shared beta capacity limits. A browser timeout stops waiting for a response; work already running on the server may continue until it finishes.
